macOS · iPhone · 数据留在本机

在 iPhone 和 Mac 上，指挥终端里的编码 agent。

Claude Code、Codex 等 agent 跑在 Herdr 窗格里。Palmhelm 把它们的会话同步到手机与 Mac，让你阅读对话、排队发消息、处理审批与提问。

下载 Mac 版 · 即将推出了解功能

Mac 版等待 Apple 公证后发布，届时在 Releases 提供安装包。

能做什么

会话同步与对话阅读

在手机与 Mac 上查看 agent 的对话。只显示用户与助手的正文，工具输出与思考不出现在这里。

消息排队与原因说明

草稿、等待空闲、排位各有说明，知道消息为什么还没发出。

立即插入

agent 正在工作时，也能把一句话插进当前回合。

审批与提问提醒

审批和提问到达时提醒你，在手机上直接处理。

局域网与 Tailscale 直连

同一局域网或经 Tailscale 直连 Mac。对话与数据在设备之间直接传输，没有 Palmhelm 自己的服务器。

Mac 内置 Bridge

菜单栏与刘海常驻，Bridge 由 App 启动与停止，不需要另外安装服务。

下载与安装

  1. 1

    系统要求 macOS 15 或更高版本。

  2. 2

    在 Releases 下载安装包，把 Palmhelm 拖到“应用程序”。

  3. 3

    首次打开 Palmhelm，然后在 iPhone 上与这台 Mac 配对。

  4. 4

    离开局域网时，用 Tailscale 连接同一台 Mac。iPhone 版即将上架 App Store。

隐私

数据留在你的 Mac。会话记录、消息与审批都保存在本机数据目录。

对话与数据在你的设备之间直接传输，没有 Palmhelm 自己的服务器。可选的飞书通道经飞书传输，iPhone 推送经 Apple 送达；Tailscale 无法直连时，会经其加密中继。

Bridge 只在局域网或 Tailscale 地址上提供接口：TLS 加密，并需要设备令牌。

macOS · iPhone · data stays on your Mac

Run the coding agents in your terminal,
from iPhone and Mac.

Claude Code, Codex and other agents run in Herdr panes. Palmhelm syncs their sessions to your phone and Mac, so you can read the conversation, queue messages, and handle approvals and questions.

Download for Mac · coming soonWhat it does

The Mac app will be published after Apple notarization, with the installer on Releases.

What it does

Session sync and reading

Read the agent's conversation on your phone and Mac. Only the user and assistant text shows; tool output and reasoning do not.

Queued messages with reasons

Drafts, waiting for idle, and queue position each explain why a message has not gone out yet.

Immediate insert

Slip a line into the current turn even while the agent is working.

Approval and question alerts

Get alerted when an approval or question arrives, and answer it on your phone.

LAN and Tailscale direct

Connect straight to your Mac on the same network or over Tailscale. Conversations and data travel directly between devices, with no Palmhelm server in between.

Built-in Bridge on Mac

Lives in the menu bar and the notch. The app starts and stops the Bridge; no separate service to install.

Download and install

  1. 1

    Requires macOS 15 or later.

  2. 2

    Download the installer from Releases and drag Palmhelm into Applications.

  3. 3

    Open Palmhelm for the first time, then pair your iPhone with this Mac.

  4. 4

    Away from your network, connect to the same Mac over Tailscale. The iPhone app is coming to the App Store.

Privacy

Your data stays on your Mac. Session records, messages and approvals are stored in the local data directory.

Conversations and data travel directly between your devices, with no Palmhelm server in between. The optional Feishu channel goes through Feishu, iPhone push notifications go through Apple, and when Tailscale cannot connect directly it uses its encrypted relay.

The Bridge serves only on LAN or Tailscale addresses: TLS encrypted, and every request needs a device token.
